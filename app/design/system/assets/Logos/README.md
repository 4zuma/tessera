# Logos

A disc (a disk platter seen from above) holding a three-tile treemap, with a hub at the centre. A tessera is one tile of a mosaic.

- `tessera.svg`: the master artwork on a 48 grid (gradients and a rim), used from 48 px up.
- `tessera-16.svg`: the 16 px artwork, flat `brand-*` colours on its own pixel grid; the `Logo` component draws the same. The sizes between (20 to 40 px) are in the repository's `docs/brand`.

Full colour on any surface in either theme: the disc brings its own rim and ring. Never tint the logo or follow the accent with it. The files are written by `scripts/brand-icons.mjs` in the repository; change the script, not the files.
