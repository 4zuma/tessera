The content dialog that guards what cannot be undone: deleting permanently and emptying the Recycle Bin. Title in `type-subtitle`, the item and its size (in `critical`), one sentence of consequence, then the destructive button (`btn danger`) and Cancel, which has the focus.

Centred over the window with `smoke` behind it. Recycling never asks; it offers Undo instead. Shown here over a `Treemap` with `scenario="confirm"`.
