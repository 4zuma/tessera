The toast at the bottom of the window, on acrylic: an icon by kind (recycle, success, info, error), an optional title, one or two lines of text and up to two actions (Undo as `btn accent`).

It lives 5 to 10 s (longer with actions); previews keep it up. Shown here after recycling a file, over a `Treemap` with `scenario="toast"`.
